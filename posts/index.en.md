**bobo** brings session notifications, quotas, device status, skills, and agents into one macOS workspace. See progress at a glance, get a reminder when needed, and click to return to work.

**[Download for macOS ↓](https://github.com/ohmyangboy/bobo/releases/latest/download/bobo.app.zip)** · [GitHub](https://github.com/ohmyangboy/bobo) · [Installation guide (中文)](/posts/getting-started/)

macOS 13.0+ · Requires Node.js 22.13+ on your Mac · Free and open source / MIT

## Notification island

![Latest notification island with session avatars, quota rings, and device indicators](assets/notch-collapsed.png?w=248&r=12)

Session status, quotas, and device indicators at the top of your screen. Hover to expand; click to return. Supports **OpenCode, Codex, Claude Code, omp, DeepSeek Harness, and Antigravity**.

## One window for your daily tools

- **Quotas**: usage and reset times for Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode Go, and Antigravity.
- **Devices**: CPU, memory, disk, processes, network rates, and service routes.
- **Agents**: install, edit, and update skills; manage agents and configuration, with AI summaries and translation.
